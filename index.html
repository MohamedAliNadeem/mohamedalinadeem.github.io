About me

I’m a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Bremen. I completed my PhD in Computer Engineering in 2026 (summa cum laude).

My research develops scalable formal verification and debugging methods for digital circuits, combining parameterized complexity with ASP, SAT, and AIG-based reasoning.

Research interests: formal verification, circuit debugging, logic synthesis, parameterized complexity, and emerging hardware technologies.
